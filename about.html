01 — About

The Work and the mind behind it

Independent · Self-directed · Interdisciplinary

Xelctha (Parth Tiwari) is an independent researcher, developer, and writer working across mathematical physics, quantum theory, computer science, and the written word. Their practice is built on a single conviction: that depth across disciplines produces sharper thinking within each of them.

They work without institutional affiliation, guided by curiosity and a commitment to rigor. Current research explores four-dimensional geometry and its relationship to quantum formalism, alongside ongoing work in software development, game design, and original music composition.

Writing — technical, philosophical, and fictional — runs through everything. Documentation clarifies systems. Essays clarify ideas. Fiction clarifies people. Each form is a different instrument for the same pursuit: understanding.

02 — Approach

Three principles behind the work.

01

Rigor first

Every claim is tested. Every idea is checked against the literature. Nothing is published before it can withstand scrutiny.

02

Breadth over depth alone

Science, code, and the arts inform each other. Cross-disciplinary work is not a distraction — it is the method.

03

Build in the open

Research, code, and writing are shared publicly from the beginning. The record matters as much as the result.
