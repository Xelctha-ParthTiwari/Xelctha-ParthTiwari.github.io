05 — Future

In Development Projects in Progress

What is being built next

01 — Overview

Projects on the horizon.

Each of the following is an active, in-progress build. They span collaboration, science communication, artificial intelligence, and software engineering. Announcements and launch details will appear here as each project moves into its next phase.

02 — Projects

Four active builds.

01

Kairos

In development

A collaborative team for people building their portfolios. Members contribute to creative and scientific projects, receive credited work, and develop a real record of achievement together. Entry is open to anyone committed to improving their craft.

02

Axionyx

In development

A scientific content channel producing tutorials, explainers, and topic deep-dives. Built on the principle that rigorous science can be communicated clearly, accurately, and without compromising the underlying detail.

03

Proxima

In development

An AI system designed for accessible generation of video, imagery, audio, and complex code. The goal is to lower the barrier between an idea and its execution — giving creators and researchers direct tools to build what they can imagine.

04

Vincet

In development

A standalone web browser built from the ground up. Designed around clarity, speed, and user control — a long-term software engineering project addressing the modern web experience from first principles.

03 — Statement

On building what does not exist yet.

The future page is a commitment. Everything listed here is not a wish but a project underway. Each will be announced, documented, and launched in its own time. Follow along through the contact page or support page for updates.
