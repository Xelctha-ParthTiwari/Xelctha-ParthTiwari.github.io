02 — Principles

Three rules behind the build.

01

Clarity over clutter

A browser should be quiet. Controls appear where they are needed and recede when they are not. Chrome serves the page — never the other way around.

02

Speed as respect

Every millisecond of latency is a small tax on attention. Vincet is written to open, render, and respond faster than the modern web expects.

03

The user owns the data

No telemetry. No trackers. No hidden calls. What a person reads, saves, or searches is theirs — not a dataset.
